My Journey

By Ronish Feb 20, 2026 Reading time: 4 min

My journey didn’t start with grand ambitions or lofty goals. It started with curiosity and a desire to make life a little easier whether that meant automating math homework in high school or figuring out how to make my first small website work.

Over time, I discovered a rhythm: mornings fueled by coffee, afternoons spent in front of a glowing rectangle and weekends exploring trails and capturing landscapes through my camera lens. There’s a balance in life that I’ve grown to appreciate between the digital world and the real one.

Coffee and Music: The True Companion

Coffee isn’t just a beverage it’s a ritual. From quiet early mornings to late night, coffee as well as songs has been a companion in my journey keeping me awake, curious and creative.

“Some days, a cup of coffee feels like the first step to conquering the world.” – Me

Mountains and Clouds Over Screens

As much as I love building things in the digital world, there’s nothing quite like stepping outside. Hiking in the mountains, feeling the crisp air and watching the sunrise, clouds moving in sky reminds me why curiosity matters not just for coding, but for life itself.

If I’m not sitting in front of a glowing rectangle, you will likely find me somewhere outside, capturing a view, savoring a coffee or just taking a moment to breathe and reflect.
